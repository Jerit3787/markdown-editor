Google Drive Integration & Data Safety

Transparent, Privacy-Preserving Google Drive Access

Markdown Editor provides an optional Google Drive integration that allows you to open, view, and save markdown documents directly to and from your personal Google Drive. We follow strict privacy standards and Google's API Services User Data Policy:

Narrow, Non-Restricted Scope We request exclusively https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This narrow scope allows the application to access only the specific files you explicitly select via the Google Picker. We cannot see, edit, or delete any other files in your Drive.
No Server Storage of Documents Document contents opened from Google Drive are loaded directly into your browser's local memory for editing. No copy of your document is ever stored on our servers or databases.
AES-256-GCM Encrypted Tokens OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and stored exclusively in secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies. Disconnecting Google Drive immediately clears the session and revokes access.
No AI Training & Never Sold Google user data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. Markdown Editor is completely free and works instantly in your browser without requiring any account, email, or password.

Where are my notes stored?

By default, notes are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB and localStorage. You can also connect GitHub or Google Drive for cloud storage.

Is Markdown Editor open source?

Yes. The entire codebase is open source under the MIT license and hosted publicly on GitHub at github.com/Jerit3787/markdown-editor.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once loaded, the core editor runs entirely client-side, allowing you to write, format, and preview your notes with zero internet connection.
